# Ultimate Dashboard Requirements for DoorDash Merchant Success

## Overview
The Ultimate dashboard is designed to provide senior administrators and managers with comprehensive insights into how Merchant Success Managers (reps) are utilizing the MSM Campaign Dispatcher tool. This dashboard should surface key metrics, trends, and alerts to help managers identify areas for improvement, recognize high performers, and ensure the tool is being used effectively.

## Dashboard Sections

### 1. Executive Summary
- **Total logins today**: Number of unique reps who logged in today.
- **Active reps**: Number of reps who have been active in the last 7 days.
- **Emails sent today**: Total number of promotional emails sent today.
- **Top performers**: List of top 5 reps by engagement score.

### 2. Rep Utilization Table
A detailed table showing per-rep metrics:
- **Last login**: Date and time of the last login.
- **Sessions today**: Number of sessions today.
- **Emails sent**: Total emails sent by the rep.
- **Promo types used**: Types of promotions used (e.g., discount, free delivery).
- **Features accessed**: List of features accessed by the rep.
- **Daily limit status**: Current status of the rep's daily limit (e.g., 50/100 emails sent).
- **Session duration avg**: Average duration of sessions in minutes.

### 3. Trends & Charts
Visual representations of key metrics over time:
- **Daily/weekly active reps line chart**: Shows the number of active reps over the last 30 days.
- **Email volume trend**: Shows the trend in the number of emails sent over time.
- **Promo type distribution pie chart**: Shows the distribution of different promo types used.
- **Feature adoption over time area chart**: Shows how the adoption of different features has changed over time.

### 4. Alerts & Exceptions Panel
A section highlighting reps who need attention:
- **Reps not logged in >3 days**: List of reps who haven't logged in for more than 3 days.
- **Reps hitting daily limits**: List of reps who are approaching or have hit their daily limits.
- **Unusual spike/drop in activity**: Alerts for any unusual changes in activity levels.

### 5. Feature Health Section
Insights into how different features are being used:
- **Feature usage**: Which features are being used the most/least.
- **Feature funnel/drop-off rates**: How reps are moving through different features and where they might be dropping off.

### 6. Team Comparison
A side-by-side comparison of rep metrics with team averages:
- **Rep metrics vs team averages**: Comparison of individual rep metrics with team averages.
- **Top and bottom performers**: Highlighting the top and bottom performers in the team.

## Specific Metrics

### 1. Adoption Rate
- **Definition**: Percentage of invited reps who have logged in at least once.
- **Purpose**: Measure the initial uptake of the tool.

### 2. Engagement Score
- **Definition**: Composite score based on session count, session duration, and feature variety.
- **Purpose**: Provide a holistic view of how engaged reps are with the tool.

### 3. Stickiness Ratio
- **Definition**: Ratio of Daily Active Users (DAU) to Monthly Active Users (MAU).
- **Purpose**: Measure how consistently reps are using the tool.

### 4. Email Volume
- **Definition**: Number of emails sent per rep, per day, segmented by promo type.
- **Purpose**: Understand the volume and type of communications being sent.

### 5. Override Usage
- **Definition**: How often reps use the "Apply to All" feature vs per-merchant edits.
- **Purpose**: Understand the balance between bulk and targeted communications.

### 6. Blank Email Tracking
- **Definition**: Track the number of blank emails sent by reps.
- **Purpose**: Identify potential misuse or bypassing of limits.

### 7. Limit Events
- **Definition**: Number of requests, approvals, denials, and daily limit hits.
- **Purpose**: Monitor how often reps are hitting their limits and the outcomes of limit requests.

## Alerts and Notifications

### 1. Rep > 3 Days Without Login
- **Action**: Flag as "Needs Check-in"
- **Purpose**: Identify reps who may need support or follow-up.

### 2. Rep on Pace to Hit Daily Limit
- **Action**: Preemptive warning
- **Purpose**: Alert managers to reps who may need additional support or limit adjustments.

### 3. Rep Sending Only Blank Emails
- **Action**: Flag as "Low Engagement"
- **Purpose**: Identify reps who may be bypassing limits or not fully utilizing the tool.

### 4. First Login of New Rep
- **Action**: Positive notification
- **Purpose**: Celebrate new reps and encourage continued use.

### 5. Rep Adopted a New Feature for First Time
- **Action**: Celebrate
- **Purpose**: Recognize and encourage the adoption of new features.

## Privacy Boundaries

### What an Ultimate Can See
- All aggregate data
- Per-rep metrics
- Trends and patterns

### What They Shouldn't See
- Actual email content (unless for audit purposes)
- Personal device details

### Team-Level Anonymization Option
- **Purpose**: Allow for company-wide reports without exposing individual rep data.

## Visualization Recommendations

### 1. Tables
- **Use Case**: For per-rep drill-down (sortable, filterable, exportable)
- **Example**: Rep Utilization Table

### 2. Line Charts
- **Use Case**: For trends over time (DAU, email volume)
- **Example**: Daily/weekly active reps line chart

### 3. Bar Charts
- **Use Case**: For rep comparisons
- **Example**: Email volume by rep

### 4. Heatmaps
- **Use Case**: For rep × feature usage matrix
- **Example**: Feature adoption heatmap

### 5. Gauge/KPI Cards
- **Use Case**: For current-day metrics
- **Example**: Executive Summary

## Utilization Scoring Approach

### 1. Composite "Utilization Score"
- **Components**: Login frequency, session duration, feature breadth, email volume, recency
- **Calculation**: Weighted combination of the above components

### 2. Tier System
- **High Utilization**: Top 20% of reps
- **Medium Utilization**: Middle 60% of reps
- **Low Utilization**: Bottom 20% of reps

### 3. Trend Arrows
- **Improving**: ↑
- **Stable**: →
- **Declining**: ↓

## Conclusion
The Ultimate dashboard should provide a comprehensive view of tool utilization by reps, highlighting key metrics, trends, and alerts. This will enable senior administrators and managers to effectively monitor and support their teams, ensuring the tool is used to its full potential.
